# Retained component notices

The files in `original_OR2/` reproduce the historical notices without changes.
They identify their original software scope and external prerequisites. They do
not assign an aggregate licence to Source Review Evidence 1.0.0 or establish that
the retained computational records have the same rights as the source component.

The source component also retains its notices in `source/`. Current distribution
identity and the component-rights review are described in
[Provenance](../../docs/PROVENANCE.md).
