[Component index](../00-kit-index.md) · [Distribution manifest](../../DISTRIBUTION_MANIFEST.json)

The catalogue includes retained maintenance utilities whose historical gates require their original inventories or environments. Their presence is not a claim that those gates have passed for this distribution.
