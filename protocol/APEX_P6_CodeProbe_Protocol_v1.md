# CodeProbe protocol v1 — bounded current-evidence route

**Status:** reconstructed retrospective protocol plus prospective Phase-6 candidate repairs. It is not a preregistration of the historical study.  
**Article type:** bounded implementation case study.  
**Principal-evidence rule:** CE09/CE11-dependent quantities are historical context until exact restoration.

## Aim and scope

The protocol tests finite obligations at six boundaries of the CodeProbe route. It supports existence, conformance and non-substitution claims within declared domains. It does not estimate natural-project prevalence, causal or human benefit, economic value, universal correctness, authorship or independent replication.

## Units and design

The current units are source artifacts, logical callable entities, observation slots, source-evidence spans, policy profiles and consumer envelopes. Cases are curated or generated purposively. The design is retrospective and implementation-informed. Assertion totals, transformations and profiles are dependent checks and are not pooled into a statistical sample.

## Gates and estimands

The six gates are specified in `FormalBoundarySpecification_v2`. Current estimands are exact gate-state vectors, full-interval/endpoint policy agreement, bounded callable-oracle agreement, identity metamorphisms and deterministic numerical statuses. Every current decision rule is conjunctive: one valid counterexample fails the corresponding obligation.

## Analysis and stopping rules

All declared cells are run. No seed, threshold, baseline, expected result or test is changed after observing an unfavourable outcome. No early stopping occurs after a pass. A failed obligation triggers repair behind a failing-before test, or reduction/removal of the claim. No p-value, population confidence interval or causal estimate is produced from the current finite programme.

## Measurement, identity and numerics

Decision-point count is ratio-scale. Offset McCabe-style complexity `C=1+d` is ordinal in candidate specification 1.2.0. `record_id` is stable observation-slot identity within one result; `measurement_digest` is deterministic content identity. Published candidate comparisons use exact decimal lexical values. Binary64 boundary overlap is indeterminate and cannot pass. These rules are prospective.

## Robustness and multiverse

The fixed multiverse spans declared threshold profiles, eligible source representations, evidence scopes, numerical representations and consumer checks. Every cell and refusal is reported. Robustness/failure radii are listed for every retained headline claim.

## Deviations and validity

All known retrospective deviations are listed in `DeviationLog_v1.csv`. The dominant limitations are implementation-informed case selection, shared CPython AST representation, absent CE09/CE11 packages and synthetic/ecological scope. Their consequences are claim restrictions rather than rhetorical caveats.

## Reproduction

The Phase-6 candidate and test harness operate offline. Phase 7 will integrate the candidate into a complete claim-carrying kit, results manifest, claims-as-tests, independent compact oracle and reviewer quick start.

## Future study

`DecisiveStudyPAP_v1` is registration-ready but unexecuted. It compares value-only, structural and six-gate routes on externally developed projects with blinded adjudication. It cannot support a current efficacy claim.
