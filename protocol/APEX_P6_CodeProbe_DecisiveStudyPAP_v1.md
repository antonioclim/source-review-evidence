# Decisive Study pre-analysis plan v1

**Status:** registration-ready; not registered and not executed.

## Decision and design

The study decides whether the complete six-gate route produces stable incremental detection beyond a strongest structural comparator. It uses a staged paired design over project × scenario units, clustered by project. B0 checks value agreement, B1 checks structure/result format and B2 applies G1–G6.

## Pilot

The pilot contains 24 scenarios from at least six externally developed Python projects, balanced between natural records and prospectively perturbed records. Scenario order and de-identified arm presentation are randomised with a retained seed before review. Two adjudicators classify outputs independently and initially blind to method labels.

## Estimands

The primary estimand is the paired proportion of adjudicated boundary defects detected by B2 and missed by B1. Secondary estimands are false-alert rate, unresolved-case rate, additional review time, yield by gate and leave-one-project-out stability. Failed or missing runs remain typed unresolved outcomes.

## Analysis

Report exact counts, the paired project-clustered estimate, a 10,000-resample project-level bootstrap interval, a Wilson interval for false alerts, full review-time distribution and leave-one-project-out estimates. There is one primary estimand. Secondary outcomes are interpreted descriptively under the predeclared continuation thresholds.

## Continuation and falsification

Continue only if B2 adds at least four adjudicated defects beyond B1, the upper 95% Wilson bound for false alerts is below 0.20, no project supplies more than half of incremental detections and median additional review time is at most ten minutes. Failure of any rule abandons the strong N2 claim for this cycle. The full stage targets a 95% half-width of at most 0.10, capped at 80 scenarios.

## Ethics and registration

An institutional ethics determination, legal project access and a frozen pilot manifest are prerequisites. The plan cannot support any current efficacy claim before execution and independent adjudication.
